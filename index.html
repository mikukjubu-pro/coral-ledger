Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need Google Sheets to use these templates?

You need a free Google account. Open the link, choose “Make a copy”, and the template is yours.

Are these beginner friendly?

Yes. Every template has a Start Here tab and you only type in your own numbers — the formulas do the rest.

Will these work on my phone or tablet?

Yes. They work in the Google Sheets app on phones and tablets, and in any browser on a laptop.

Can I get a refund?

These are digital files you get right away, so all sales are final.

Can I share a template with my partner?

Yes. Use the Share button in Google Sheets to budget together on the same copy.

Do I pay every month?

No. You pay once and keep your copy for as long as you like.
